TaskBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

TaskBuddy is a private tool used by a single family. Only that family's own Google account signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, TaskBuddy uses the Google Drive and Google Sheets APIs to upload the family's own files to their Drive and to read and update their own spreadsheets.

What it does with that data

The data is used only to keep the family's own records. TaskBuddy does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the family's own computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TaskBuddy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
